D.1: wide tree, down to the measure

D with a third level. The tree goes category → subcategory → measure (Macroeconomics → General → GDP), and the measure is the leaf: choosing it opens the app’s own Build choices for it, run on the real datasets. No codes.

Browse stays as it is in the app: the search field and the catalogue of every dataset by subject. Build walks a structure down to a measure and its choices.

What changed from D

  • Three levels. The two levels the structure prints (section → subsection, or major topic → subtopic), then GovGraph’s measures as the leaves. A subcategory folds its measures; an empty one has none and stays grey.
  • The leaf opens the real Build choices (dimensions.js copied from the client, the series and dimensions from the local database), so GDP shows “Measured as: Amount / Growth rate”, then how often, years and source, as in the app.
  • No codes for CAP’s topics, and the crumb names the category and subcategory above the measure.
  • The filter matches categories, subcategories, measures and dataset names; a measure listed only for a dataset name shows the names that matched.
  • “N on” sits on the measure, and on a category or subcategory when it is folded. The unemployment rate and the home price index start on.
  • Placements are still a proposal: in the Abstract they wait for the table list (Phase 2); in CAP every one is GovGraph’s judgement, since CAP sorts documents, not statistics.
